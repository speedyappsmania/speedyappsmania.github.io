Frequently Asked Questions

🎮 The app keeps crashing — what should I do?
First, try force-closing and reopening the app. If that doesn't help, go to Settings → Apps → [App Name] → Clear Cache and try again. Still not working? Email us with your device model and Android version.
📶 Can I play without an internet connection?
Yes! Our puzzle games are designed to work offline. You'll need an internet connection for ads to load, but gameplay itself works without one.
🔒 What data does the app collect?
We don't collect personal data directly. Our ad partner Google AdMob may collect non-personal device data for ad targeting. See each app's Privacy Policy for full details.
🚫 How do I turn off personalised ads?
On your Android device, go to Settings → Google → Ads → Opt out of Ads Personalisation. This works across all apps that use Google AdMob.
💾 I lost my progress — can you help?
Game progress is saved locally on your device. If you uninstalled and reinstalled the app, the progress may have been lost. Email us and we'll see what we can do.
